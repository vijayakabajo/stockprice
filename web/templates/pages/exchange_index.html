What is a company's primary exchange?

A company's primary exchange is the stock exchange where its shares are listed and where it must meet that exchange's listing standards.

A listed stock can also trade on other exchanges and venues, but it has one primary listing. Nasdaq and the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE) are the primary listing for most U.S. companies; NYSE American lists mainly smaller companies. Each exchange page above ranks its listed companies by market cap.
